What sits behind every one of those concessions has a name: the focus lag. It is the measurable delay between where your eye points and where your vision actually resolves. Your retina — a strip of tissue thinner than a credit card — burns more metabolic fuel per gram than your brain does. It runs without pause, converting light into signal all day, and every photon costs nutrients. When the supply line thins, the retina does not fail. It slows. Transitions take longer, fine detail gets softer, and fatigue arrives hours earlier than it used to.

Visium Pro Ingredients

Six ingredients, named individually, no proprietary blend obscuring the amounts. Every one of them addresses a specific job inside the eye.

  • Vitamin A: The raw material for rhodopsin — the pigment that makes low-light vision possible. When rhodopsin runs low, dim rooms get dimmer and headlights at night start to scatter. This is the nutrient your retina reaches for first.
  • Vitamin C: The eye concentrates more vitamin C than almost any other tissue in the body. That concentration exists to neutralize oxidative damage from a lifetime of light exposure — every waking hour, every wavelength.
  • Vitamin E: Partners with vitamin C against the slow oxidative wear that accumulates in tissue that never gets to rest. The two work as a relay: vitamin C handles the water-soluble side, vitamin E covers the fat-soluble membranes.
  • Zinc: Concentrated in the retina specifically, where it does two things — maintains retinal structure and transports vitamin A from the liver to the eye. Without zinc, the vitamin A in this formula cannot reach the tissue that needs it.
  • Selenium: Supports the enzymes that recycle spent antioxidants back into active form. Without it, the protective chain above runs down faster than it rebuilds.
  • Chromium: The inclusion that shows the formula was thought through rather than assembled from a list. Retinal health depends on the smallest blood vessels in the body, and those vessels depend on steady glucose. Chromium supports balanced blood sugar — not an obvious eye nutrient until you understand the vascular connection.

The formula is deliberately conservative. These are established nutrients with decades of research behind them, chosen because they work rather than because they sound novel.
